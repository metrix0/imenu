# iMenu Plus: product and access map

Read this document before changing plan features, names, pricing, access or billing. Keep it current with the same change.

## Product definition

`lib/addons/products.ts` is the source of truth for the name, price, three main benefits and four Plus bonuses. The customer-facing name is **iMenu Plus** (formerly IA Plus). The price remains R$ 49,99/month. Marketing's full feature name is **Automações de Marketing WhatsApp**, under `/painel/marketing`.

Main benefits, in order: Automações de Marketing WhatsApp; Assistente IA; Vendas IA. The iMenu Plus product card subtitle is **Venda mais com iMenu Plus.** (no unverified sales multiplier claims), and its main-benefits list is displayed without a "Recursos principais" heading.

Plus bonuses, in order: Atendimento exclusivo; Upsells automáticos; Atualizações de pedidos pelo WhatsApp; Download Cardápio em PDF. Order updates require the established WhatsApp interaction and deduplication rules. PDF export is exclusive to valid iMenu Plus access; free QR does not unlock it. Scan de Cardápio com IA, Modo vitrine and Painel Garçom are free features, not Plus bonuses. This presentation does not remove existing free access to other features; the existing Assistente IA free allowance and automatic upsell behavior are preserved.

## Compatibility and entitlement

- Keep the database/payment key `ia_plus`, provider reference prefix `ia-plus`, `/api/ia-plus/*`, activation event `imenu:ia-plus-activated` and existing terms URL `/restaurante/dados/termos/ia-plus`. They identify existing subscriptions and integrations; they are not customer-facing plan names.
- `lib/addons/access.ts` checks Plus access. Existing paid-period semantics apply: active subscriptions, or canceled/past-due subscriptions with a future paid-period end, retain access. An inactive, pending or expired subscription does not grant it.
- QR Code Mesa is free for every restaurant, independently of iMenu Plus. It creates no paid add-on or payment. `public.restaurant_qr_settings` stores its universal token and design. The authenticated `/api/qr-table/activate` initializes only that configuration; visiting Mesas never rewrites legacy prices, status, paid periods or receipts.
- Existing QR/Plus tokens and designs are copied into free settings, preferring the historical QR record. Public menus and order validation also accept every historical universal token and each active table's public token regardless of subscription status. Expiring/canceling Plus cannot invalidate printed QR codes.
- `restaurant_addons.qr_code_mesa` and its payments remain historical records only. New paid QR checkout and reconciliation routes return 410. Previously issued Mercado Pago payments are validated against historical QR prices (not the new zero product price) and retained for accounting. QR purchase/expiry messages are not sent.
- Legacy QR provider renewals are stopped at the provider and their local records synchronized without deleting payment history or issuing refunds. Historical successful positive QR payments establish PROMOQR eligibility independently of the free configuration or canceled subscription status.
- `restaurant_tables` owner SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE RLS policies require ownership only. `restaurant_qr_settings` enables RLS with owner-only SELECT; writes use the owner-authenticated server endpoints. Never commit SQL files.
