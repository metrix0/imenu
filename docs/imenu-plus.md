# iMenu Plus: product and access map

Read this document before changing plan features, names, pricing, access or billing. Keep it current with the same change.

## Product definition

`lib/addons/products.ts` is the source of truth for the name, price, three main benefits and six Plus bonuses. The customer-facing name is **iMenu Plus** (formerly IA Plus). The price remains R$ 49,99/month. Marketing's full feature name is **Automações de Marketing WhatsApp**, under `/painel/marketing`.

Main benefits, in order: Automações de Marketing WhatsApp; Assistente IA; Vendas IA. The iMenu Plus product card subtitle is **Venda mais com iMenu Plus.** (no unverified sales multiplier claims), and its main-benefits list is displayed without a "Recursos principais" heading.

Plus bonuses, in order: Atendimento exclusivo; Upsells automáticos; Modo vitrine; Atualizações de pedidos pelo WhatsApp; Scan de cardápio com IA; Download Cardápio em PDF. Order updates require the established WhatsApp interaction and deduplication rules; menu scanning remains free with zero or one item, then requires Plus. This presentation does not remove existing free access to other features; the existing Assistente IA free allowance and automatic upsell behavior are preserved.

## Compatibility and entitlement

- Keep the database/payment key `ia_plus`, provider reference prefix `ia-plus`, `/api/ia-plus/*`, activation event `imenu:ia-plus-activated` and existing terms URL `/restaurante/dados/termos/ia-plus`. They identify existing subscriptions and integrations; they are not customer-facing plan names.
- `lib/addons/access.ts` checks Plus access. Existing paid-period semantics apply: active subscriptions, or canceled/past-due subscriptions with a future paid-period end, retain access. An inactive, pending or expired subscription does not grant it.
- QR Code Mesa is free for every restaurant, independently of iMenu Plus. Plus does not create or require an additional paid QR subscription, synchronization trigger, duplicate payment or backfill row. Plus cancellation/expiry does not remove free QR access.
- `lib/qr-table/types.ts` resolves existing QR records and preserves their universal token and design independently of paid-period expiry. Plus-only subscribers can still use the Plus record's universal token and design. Previously printed per-table and universal QR codes remain valid.
- QR-only is free with no expiry or recurring charge for new subscriptions. Historical paid QR subscriptions and payments are retained in the database for compatibility; they are not automatically canceled or refunded. Do not present QR as a paid subscription or display its historical records in the plan-management billing UI. Legacy checkout/payment endpoints remain for compatibility, not for selling new QR subscriptions.
- `restaurant_tables` owner SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE RLS policies accept either valid `qr_code_mesa` or `ia_plus` access, while retaining ownership checks. Update these policies through iMenu Supabase when changing entitlement rules; do not commit SQL files.
